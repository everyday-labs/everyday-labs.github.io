Turn warehouse trips into a game

Every warehouse visit can earn a star. Stars build toward five fan tiers and unlock collectible badges, a small extra on top of a trip you were taking anyway.

📍

Arrive at a warehouse

Open the app once you're there. Your location is checked only when you tap, never tracked in the background.

✅

Tap "Check In"

The app confirms you're within about 50 meters of the warehouse before the visit counts.

⭐

Earn a star

One star per warehouse per day, so repeat check-ins don't add up.

🧾

Forgot to check in? Scan the receipt

A scanned receipt proves the visit, so it earns the same star, dated to the day of the trip. A second receipt from the same trip doesn't double it.
