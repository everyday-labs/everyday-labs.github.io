Never miss a price drop

Costco refunds the difference if something you bought drops in price within 30 days. Most people never notice in time. Bulkmate notices for you.

1

Scan your receipt

Point the camera at the receipt after checkout. No signal? The scan is saved and uploads once you're back online.

2

Every item gets logged

Each line is read: item, price, and any discount already applied, so your purchase history builds itself. Misread an item? Tap it to fix it.

3

Prices are watched for 30 days

Every night, current pricing is checked for everything you bought in the last 30 days, from live pricing data and from what other Bulkmate users recently paid in the warehouse.

4

You're told right away, receipt ready

A drop sends a push notification, an email, and an alert in the app. Tap it to pull up the original receipt to bring to the membership counter.

The 30-day window

The whole feature is built around one rule: a purchase stays eligible for a price adjustment for 30 days after you buy it.

🔁

One alert per drop

You hear about each drop once. You're only told again if the price falls further, and an alert you dismissed comes back in that case.

🔕

Your choice of alerts

Push and email can each be turned off in Profile → Preferences. Every email also has a one-click unsubscribe link.

✏️

Fix a misread

Receipt scanning isn't perfect. If a price or item name looks wrong, tap the line and correct it.

💵

Two price sources

The price used is the lower of live pricing data and the median that other Bulkmate users recently paid for the same item.

🗂

Full history, searchable

Every receipt is saved and searchable by item, date range, warehouse, or amount, so nothing is lost with the paper copy.

🏬

Matched to your warehouse

Receipts are matched to one of 720+ warehouses worldwide. If a receipt can't be matched, pick the warehouse from a list.
