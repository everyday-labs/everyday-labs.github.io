Bulkmate

A free companion app for Costco members, by Everyday Labs. It keeps track of what you buy, watches for price drops so you can claim the refund, and turns warehouse trips into a small collecting game along the way.

Independent and open source · not affiliated with or endorsed by Costco Wholesale
🧾

Receipts & Price Alerts

Scan a receipt and every item is logged. If a price drops within 30 days, you get a push notification and an email, because Costco refunds the difference.

📦

Barcode Scanner

Scan any product in the warehouse to see its price, what other shoppers paid, and a plain-language ingredient check.

Part 3 →
⭐

Check-Ins & Rewards

Check in at a warehouse, or scan that trip's receipt, to earn stars, climb five fan tiers, and unlock collectible badges.

Part 4 →
📊

Spend Analytics

A dashboard of where your Costco money goes: monthly trends, top items, and how much you've saved.

Part 5 →

🔐 Sign in your way

Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Forgot a password? Reset it with a code sent to your inbox.

✉️ Alerts by email

Price-drop emails arrive as soon as a drop is found. Turn them off in the app or with one click from any email.

🗑 Your data stays yours

Delete any receipt, or your whole account, from inside the app. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.
