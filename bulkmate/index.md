**Bulkmate** is a companion app for warehouse-club shoppers, by Everyday Labs. It exists
because receipts get lost and price-adjustment windows get missed.

### What it does

- **Receipt tracking.** Photograph a Costco receipt and Bulkmate reads every item, price, and
  discount, and keeps a searchable history with spending analytics.
- **Price-drop alerts.** If something you bought in the last 30 days drops in price, you get a
  notification so you can ask for a price adjustment.
- **Barcode lookup.** Scan a product to see current pricing and an at-a-glance ingredient
  breakdown (good / watch / avoid), using open data from Open Food Facts.
- **Check-ins and badges.** Check in at a warehouse to earn stars, level up your member tier,
  and collect badges for the places you visit.

### Status

Bulkmate is in **beta** on iOS. Android is planned.
