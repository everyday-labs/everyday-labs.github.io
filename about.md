**Everyday Labs** is an independent, non-commercial studio that builds small, focused,
open-source apps.

Every project starts the same way: something in everyday life is more tedious than it should
be, so a tool gets built to fix it. When it proves useful, it gets polished and shared.

### What Everyday Labs is

- A home for small, focused, open-source apps.
- A place to learn by building real things, not toy demos.
- Independent, and run in public.

### What it isn't

- A startup chasing growth, or a business selling your data.
- Affiliated with the companies whose products its apps work with. Bulkmate, for example,
  reads Costco receipts, but it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Costco
  Wholesale Corporation.
