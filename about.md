I go by **Tinker** — a developer and tech enthusiast who builds side projects in spare time.
(Yes, named after the Dota 2 hero. It seemed fitting.)

**Everyday Labs** is simply the name for that work. Each project starts the same way: something
in my own day is more tedious than it should be, so I build a tool to fix it. If it turns out to
be useful, I polish it and share it.

### What Everyday Labs is

- A home for small, focused, open-source apps.
- A place to learn by building real things, not toy demos.
- Run by one person, in public.

### What it isn't

- A startup chasing growth, or a business selling your data.
- Affiliated with any of the companies whose products my apps work with. Bulkmate, for example,
  reads Costco receipts, but it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Costco
  Wholesale Corporation.
