**Everyday Labs** builds small, useful, open-source apps for everyday problems.

It isn't a company in the usual sense. There are no investors, no ads, and nothing to upsell.
Each app starts with an everyday annoyance, is built to be genuinely useful, and is shared
openly so anyone can use it, read the code, or improve it.

## Projects

### [Bulkmate](/bulkmate/)

A companion app for warehouse-club shoppers. Snap a receipt and Bulkmate tracks what was
bought, warns when a price drops within the price-adjustment window, looks up ingredients from
a barcode scan, and turns warehouse visits into a little collection game.
*iOS · in beta · open source*

[Learn more](/bulkmate/) · [Source code](https://github.com/everyday-labs) · [Privacy](/bulkmate/privacy/) · [Support](/bulkmate/support/)

## Principles

- **Open source by default.** Code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/everyday-labs) under
  the MIT license.
- **Your data is yours.** Apps collect only what they need, state exactly what that is, and let
  you delete it from inside the app.
- **Built to be useful, not sticky.** No dark patterns, no engagement tricks.
