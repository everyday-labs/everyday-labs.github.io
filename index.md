Hi — I'm **Tinker**. Everyday Labs is the name I put on the small apps I build in my spare time.

It's not a company in the usual sense. There are no investors, no ads, and nothing to upsell.
I build things when an everyday annoyance bugs me enough, I make them as good as I can, and I
share them openly so anyone can use them, read the code, or improve them.

## Projects

### [Bulkmate](/bulkmate/)

A companion app for warehouse-club shoppers. Snap your receipt and Bulkmate tracks what you
bought, warns you when a price drops within the price-adjustment window, looks up ingredients
when you scan a barcode, and turns warehouse visits into a little collection game.
*iOS · in beta · open source*

[Learn more](/bulkmate/) · [Source code](https://github.com/everyday-labs) · [Privacy](/bulkmate/privacy/) · [Support](/bulkmate/support/)

## How I work

- **Open source by default.** Code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/everyday-labs) under
  the MIT license.
- **Your data is yours.** Apps collect only what they need, say exactly what that is, and let
  you delete it from inside the app.
- **Built to be useful, not sticky.** No dark patterns, no engagement tricks.
